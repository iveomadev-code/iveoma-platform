## Section D — /impact vault card: "Strengthening Rural Health Systems Through Community-Led Models"

**Filter tag:** Health Systems
**Current location:** "Ebonyi State"

**REPLACE card content with:**

**Title:** When the World Stopped, Iveoma Stepped Forward

**Location:** Okposi and surrounding communities, Ebonyi State

**Year:** 2020

**Body text:**
In 2020, the COVID-19 pandemic triggered unprecedented disruption across the world — and Ebonyi State was no exception. Government-mandated sit-at-home orders, while necessary to curb the spread of the virus, brought daily economic life to a halt. For countless residents — traders, artisans, subsistence earners — the absence of income was not an inconvenience. It was a survival crisis.

The Iveoma Development Network mounted a targeted relief effort, taking concrete, coordinated action across four fronts:

**Handwash basins** — Installed at schools and churches across Okposi, the two most attended communal spaces, to promote basic hygiene as the first line of defence against transmission.

**Hand sanitizers** — Distributed to institutions across Ebonyi State, ensuring that even communities without running water had access to hygienic protection.

**Face masks** — Supplied to students, church members, and the general public in communities where access to PPE was severely limited.

**Emergency cash relief** — Provided direct cash support to indigent persons in Ebonyi State whose livelihoods had been disrupted by the government-sanctioned sit-at-home period.

**Pull quote:**
> A network that only shows up in good times is not truly a network. Iveoma's response in 2020 was a demonstration of solidarity — not charity, but the recognition that the community's pain belongs to all of us.

**Title:** Building Legacies — The Sir & Lady Nwani Chuku Hall

**Location:** Holy Rosary Secondary School, Okposi, Ohaozara LGA, Ebonyi State

**Year:** 2021

**Body text:**
In 2021, the Iveoma Development Network achieved a landmark milestone in its ongoing commitment to community upliftment: the completion, dedication, and consecration of a brand-new assembly hall at Holy Rosary Secondary School, proudly named the Sir & Lady Nwani Chuku Hall in honour of a family whose generosity made it all possible.

For years, Holy Rosary Secondary School had operated without an adequate central hall — a gathering space where students could assemble, celebrate achievements, host debates, and forge the communal bonds that make school life meaningful beyond the classroom. The IDN identified this gap and responded decisively.

The hall was formally consecrated by Bishop Michael Okoro of the Catholic Diocese of Abakaliki on 13 July 2021, in a ceremony attended by students, staff, community leaders, and well-wishers. The blessing transformed the building from infrastructure into a living symbol of faith, sacrifice, and collective ambition — a place made meaningful not just by prayer, but by the intent to educate.
