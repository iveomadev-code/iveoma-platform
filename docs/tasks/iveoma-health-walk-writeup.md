# Walking Together, Building Tomorrow
## The Iveoma Annual Health Walk — 2021 to 2025

**Category:** Health Systems & Community Wellbeing
**Location:** Okposi, Ohaozara Local Government Area, Ebonyi State
**Frequency:** Annual — every December
**Editions to date:** Five (2021 — 2025)

---

Some things announce themselves loudly. Others simply show up, year after year, until they become part of the texture of a place.

The Iveoma Annual Health Walk is the second kind.

Since 2021, every December, the streets of Okposi have filled with people walking. Community members, friends, family — those who live here and those who have come back to be here. No grand staging. No corporate sponsorship banners. Just a town in motion, together, for the simple and serious reason that health is worth showing up for.

The walk began in the same year the Iveoma Development Network consecrated the Sir and Lady Nwani Chuku Hall at Holy Rosary College — 2021. That coincidence of timing was not accidental. The health walk and the infrastructure work are expressions of the same conviction: that a community's wellbeing is built incrementally, through sustained presence, not one-off gestures.

---

## What the walk is

The Iveoma Annual Health Walk is a community health event held in Okposi each December. It promotes physical fitness, preventive health awareness, and the idea that health is a shared responsibility — not just a matter for hospitals and clinics, but for communities that choose to move together.

It is also, in a specific sense, a statement about who belongs. Members of the Okposi diaspora — people who have built lives elsewhere in Nigeria and abroad — return for the walk. It is one of the occasions that makes the thread between Okposi and its people living elsewhere visible and physical.

Brigadier General Igwe Omoke serves as Director General of the Iveoma Health Walk — a role that signals the event's seriousness as an organised, structured annual programme, not an informal gathering.

---

## Five editions, five years

**2021 — The first walk**
The inaugural edition established the format: a community walk through Okposi, drawing participants from the town and beyond. Held in December, in a year already marked by the Holy Rosary College hall consecration and the recognition Dr. Nkata received from the Colleges of Education Academic Staff Union for his contributions to public health.

**2022 — Growing in number**
The second edition saw the walk grow in participation. Video documentation from this year shows a larger crowd — an early sign that the event was taking root in the community's December calendar.

**2023 — The third year**
By the third edition, the Annual Health Walk had become a fixture. The gathering spilled beyond walking — documentation from 2023 shows the community continuing the day's momentum together, a sign of an event that had found its rhythm.

**2024 — The fourth edition**
The fourth walk continued the tradition, drawing participants in the same Okposi streets that had become familiar ground for the event.

**2025 — The 5th edition and a milestone**
The fifth edition was the most significant. Held on 29 December 2025 — the same day as the groundbreaking of the Sir Nwani and Lady Akanele Chuku Learning and Development Centre at Agunabani Village — the walk coincided with Dr. Nkata's 50th birthday.

The convergence was deliberate. A man turning fifty chose to mark the occasion not with a private celebration but by walking his hometown's streets with his community in the morning and breaking ground on a building for its young people in the afternoon. Traditional ruler of Okposi Okwu, His Royal Highness Eze Onyibe Chukwuagu, was present at the groundbreaking that followed.

The event was reported by Independent Newspaper Nigeria on 31 December 2025.

## Why it matters

A single health walk is an event. Five consecutive annual health walks is a programme. The distinction is important.

Dr. Nkata Nwani Chuku is a physician and public health specialist whose professional career has been built around one question: how do you get health systems to work for people who are most often left out of them? His work at FHI 360, KPMG West Africa, the London School of Economics, and the Federal Ministry of Health has all been oriented toward that question at scale.

The Annual Health Walk answers the same question at a different scale — in Okposi, on foot, in December, every year. The founder of a public health consultancy that has managed hundreds of millions of dollars in health portfolios chooses to spend one day a year walking through a town of a few thousand people to remind them — and himself — that health starts with community.

That is not incidental to the larger work of Iveoma Development Network. It is the foundation of it.

> "We want people around Okposi to begin to see how they can add value to the community, how Okposi can be better off by the little grace that God has given us, and by the little funding that God has given to us. When we begin to do that, opportunities in the future will be better for it."
>
> — Brigadier General Igwe Omoke, Director General, Iveoma Annual Health Walk, December 2025

---

## The walk in the wider picture

The Annual Health Walk does not exist in isolation. It is one strand in a pattern of community investment that runs from 2016 to the present — from an examination hall at Isi Okposi Girls Secondary School to a medical centre at Veritas University in Abuja to the Learning and Development Centre now rising in Agunabani Village.

What the walk adds to that pattern is continuity of presence. Buildings are commissioned once. Walks happen every year. Together, they make the argument that the Iveoma Development Network's commitment to Okposi is not episodic — it is recurring, embodied, and alive.
